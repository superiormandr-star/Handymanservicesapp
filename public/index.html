QUESTIONS?

The essentials,
answered.

Is this a booking service?

No. Business profiles link you directly to the provider. Availability, estimates and agreements are arranged with the business.

Can customers leave ratings?

Yes. A rating and review form is available on each business profile. Submitted reviews are screened before public display.

What does a $25 membership cover?

Level 1 is a planned directory listing membership. Enrollment and checkout are paused while billing is finalized. Level 2 estimate assistance is an optional upcoming service with pricing to be announced; it is not included in the directory plan.

Can a business choose which areas it serves?

Yes. During signup, select cities, counties or ZIP codes, choose a travel radius from your ZIP code, or list states. Those details are saved with your application and reviewed before publishing.

Can I add this site to my phone's home screen?

Yes, after it is published with HTTPS. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
