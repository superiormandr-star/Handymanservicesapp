Privacy information

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

Information collected

If you submit a review, we collect the name, rating, comments, date, and business reviewed. If you request a provider listing, we collect the business name, city, email, and any phone number you provide. We receive a PayPal subscription reference if you finish subscription authorization. Payment details are handled by PayPal and are not stored on this website.

Use and storage

Submitted information is used to review and manage business listings, moderate customer feedback, and contact interested advertisers. Approved reviews and business profile details are published in the directory. Other submissions are not publicly displayed. The app uses ordinary server logs and a basic rate limit to reduce spam. A hosting provider may process data when the website is deployed.

Outside services

PayPal processes subscriptions under PayPal's own privacy terms. This site links to businesses' websites and may contact them through your phone or SMS application. The app stores some content for offline use if installed on a supported phone.

Requests and questions

Contact the Handyman Services directory owner through the published business contact information to request correction or deletion of information. The owner must review requests and respond. This document describes the included application code; hosting-specific disclosures should be reviewed before launch.
