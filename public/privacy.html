Superior ProConnect Privacy Information

Version 2026-11-16 · Effective November 16, 2026

Information collected

Customer job posts collect a title, scope, general location, job type and chosen estimate limit, plus private contact details such as name, email, phone and optional full address. Private customer access links are issued; the system stores a hash of the access key. Professional profiles can include name, services, service area, contact details, professional access credentials, estimates and payment references. Reviews, membership-interest forms, job decisions, consent version/timestamps and owner moderation records may also be kept.

Who can see a job

Eligible professionals with private access may see job scope and general location but not a posting customer's private contact details. The customer may privately review verified estimates. Only the professional selected by the customer through the job workflow receives the customer's private contact details. The directory owner may access information needed for account administration, safety review, and PayPal receipt verification. Public business directory listings and approved reviews are visible to visitors; the customer's private job-management link is not a public listing.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used to operate job matching, estimate limits and access, membership review, fraud prevention, user support, moderation and backups. We do not authorize sale, recycling or resale of private customer information to other businesses. Hosting, storage and payment processors may handle limited information to operate these services. PayPal processes its own payment details; transaction references can be stored to confirm a professional's estimate fee. Completed-job service payments are handled directly between customer and professional, not through the network.

Security, retention and user choices

Access keys should be kept confidential. The platform uses owner-controlled access, restricted professional job data and persistent encrypted record storage where configured; no security system is absolute. Information may be kept as reasonably necessary for the service, legitimate recordkeeping, protection from fraud, and applicable law. Users can ask the network owner to correct or delete eligible information and can close a posted job from their private link. Deletion requests may be subject to legal or recordkeeping obligations.

Reviews, links and contact

Approved customer reviews, star ratings and business profiles can appear publicly. Third-party business websites and PayPal have their own terms and practices. For privacy questions, correction or deletion requests, or suspected misuse, contact the Superior ProConnect owner through the published business contact information.

This description must be checked against production processing, retention practices and applicable California privacy requirements before broader paid launch.
