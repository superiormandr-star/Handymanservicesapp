Rules & Regulations

Standards & Accountability · Version 2026-11-16 · Effective November 16, 2026

Superior ProConnect connects customers with independent construction, maintenance, remodeling and automotive service professionals. These standards apply to customers and professionals using the network. Both sides deserve accurate information, respectful conduct and control of their private information.

1. Customer job postings and estimates

Customers may post a job at no charge and choose a maximum of one, two or three estimates. A fourth is never automatic: after three verified estimates the customer may explicitly authorize one additional estimate. Reaching the limit, selecting a professional, or closing the job stops new estimates. Posting is not a promise to hire, and the lowest estimate need not be chosen. Customers must describe real jobs truthfully, avoid posting contact information in public job descriptions, and must not misuse the network for spam, harassment or fraudulent requests.

2. Customer privacy and professional contact

Professionals initially see job type, general location, scope and available estimate slots—not a customer's private name, telephone, email or street address. Those details are released through the job system only to the professional whose verified estimate the customer selects. Do not publish, share, copy, recycle or resell customer information; do not try to obtain private customer details outside the authorized process. Customer job links and professional access keys must be kept private. No unrestricted public messaging is offered as part of private bidding.

3. Professional integrity and legal requirements

Professionals must provide accurate business names, services, locations, credentials and qualifications. They are responsible for any licenses, registrations, insurance, permits and other legal requirements that apply to the work and jurisdiction. A listing is not a certification or guarantee by Superior ProConnect. No false reviews, impersonation, discriminatory harassment, bait-and-switch pricing, spam, hidden conditions or misleading qualifications are permitted.

4. Estimates, fees and selection

Professionals write and are responsible for their own detailed estimates, scope, exclusions, price and any conditions. Estimate submission is private. Only owner-verified membership credits or independently verified one-time PayPal estimate fees permit delivery to a customer. Under the current workflow the fee after available credits is $18 for Construction or $10 for Automotive, disclosed before submission; verification is not automatic. Submission does not guarantee a job. Any separate approved-lead fee must be specifically disclosed before any obligation; the current workflow does not charge a percentage of completed-job revenue.

5. Independent work, invoicing and disputes

Superior ProConnect operates the connection and initial estimate marketplace. Independent service professionals—not the network—decide final contract terms, carry out the work, issue invoices, collect payments from customers and manage work-related warranties, refunds and disputes. Superior ProConnect does not collect payment for completed service jobs or guarantee workmanship, schedules, safety, licensing, or outcomes. Both parties should confirm credentials and use a written agreement before work starts.

6. Conduct, complaints and enforcement

Customers and professionals must communicate respectfully, honor commitments, protect account access and report suspected fraud or misconduct to the Superior ProConnect owner through the site's published business contact. Depending on severity and evidence, the network may investigate, warn, restrict, suspend or terminate access and remove listings, posts or reviews. Serious fraud, threats or privacy violations may warrant immediate restriction. Affected users may request a review of a decision and provide relevant records; the owner will review appeals without promising reinstatement.

7. Subscriptions, cancellation and refunds

Membership packages, billing frequency, renewal amounts and cancellation options must be reviewed before any paid signup. A billing or subscription change is not effective merely because someone submits a payment reference: the owner must verify it. Billing, cancellation and refund eligibility are governed by the applicable disclosed terms and law. Violations may lead to suspension or termination, but no statement here removes any refund or other rights that applicable law protects.

8. Acceptance, updates and contact

When customers post a job or professionals submit a private estimate, they must actively accept these standards and the linked Terms and Privacy information. The application records the acceptance time and policy version for new submissions. We may revise these rules prospectively; material updates should be communicated before renewed acceptance is required. For correction, deletion, complaints or appeals, contact the Superior ProConnect owner using the business contact information published on the site.

These operating rules are presented for transparency and should receive California legal review before paid member enrollment is activated. They do not replace applicable laws.
