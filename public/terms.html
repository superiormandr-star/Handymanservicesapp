Directory terms

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

Business listings

Superior ProConnect is an independent advertising directory, not an employer or booking agent. Customers contact providers directly. A listing does not represent an endorsement, license verification, insurance verification, or work guarantee. Confirm credentials and the written scope of work before hiring.

Advertising and payments

The advertised listing price is $25 per month, subject to the actual plan shown by PayPal. Advertisers must check PayPal's price, renewal, and cancellation terms before authorizing a subscription. Payments do not automatically activate listings: submissions require review and approval. For subscription management or cancellation, use PayPal's subscription management tools.

Customer reviews

Reviews must reflect genuine personal experiences, be respectful, and avoid private information about others. Reviews may be moderated, withheld, or removed for spam, abuse, or suspected manipulation. A published review is the opinion of the reviewer, not a factual endorsement by the directory.

Updates

Directory features, listing policies, and these terms may change as the service develops. A final legal review is recommended before accepting paying advertisers.
