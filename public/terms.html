Superior ProConnect Terms of Use

Version 2026-11-16 · Effective November 16, 2026

Superior ProConnect provides a directory and a customer-controlled, private job-posting and estimate marketplace for Construction and Automotive services. By posting a job or submitting a bid, you agree to these terms, the Rules & Regulations / Standards & Accountability and the Privacy information. Use of the network is subject to applicable law.

Customers and posted jobs

Customers may post genuine requests free of charge and set an estimate cap of one, two or three. After three verified bids, a fourth requires explicit customer authorization. Customers retain the choice of whom, if anyone, to hire. Job posting, receiving an estimate and selecting a professional do not create a contract with Superior ProConnect for the performance of the work.

Independent professionals

Each professional is an independent business responsible for truthful listings, lawful licensing and insurance where required, the completeness and accuracy of estimates, written work contracts, final prices, permits, project performance, invoicing and payment collection. Superior ProConnect does not employ listed professionals, certify every credential or guarantee their work. Customers should independently check qualifications and written terms.

Fees and membership

Current professional-bid credits are subject to owner-verified membership status. Available credits are applied first; after credits run out, currently displayed one-time per-estimate fees are $18 Construction and $10 Automotive, subject to independent PayPal receipt verification before the estimate reaches a customer. A paid estimate does not guarantee selection. New recurring membership checkout is disabled until the associated PayPal subscription lifecycle is verified. Existing subscriptions retain their applicable terms. Review billing, renewal, cancellation and refund conditions before any payment. Superior ProConnect does not collect payment for completed jobs and does not take a percentage of contracted work.

Privacy and communication

Private customer contact information is released to a professional through the job workflow only after the customer chooses that professional's verified bid. Users must safeguard private job links and access credentials, and must not resell customer leads or publish personal contact details. Additional direct contact that a visitor independently initiates through a public business directory listing is separate from the private bidding process.

Reviews, prohibited activity and enforcement

Reviews must reflect authentic experiences and may be moderated for abuse, spam, private information or manipulation. Fraud, harassment, deception, unlawful solicitations and misuse of customer information are prohibited. We may issue warnings, investigate complaints, suspend access or remove accounts or posts, subject to applicable law. Users may request reconsideration by contacting the network owner. Any refund or other statutory rights remain unaffected by these terms.

Disputes and changes

Customers and professionals negotiate and resolve contract, workmanship, scheduling and service-payment disagreements with each other; the network may review abuse reports but is not the contractor or service-payment intermediary. These terms may change with a new stated version and effective date. Contact the network owner through the published business contact details for questions, complaints or data requests.
